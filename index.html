Buyer's Guide

Which Pixel Pitch Do You Need?

Rule of thumb: minimum viewing distance (in feet) ≈ pixel pitch (in mm) × 3.3

Pixel PitchMin. Viewing DistanceIndoorOutdoorTypical Use
P1.2 – P1.94–6 ft✔—Studios, boardrooms, high-end retail
P2.58 ft✔—Churches, auditoriums, lobbies
P2.9 – P3.910–13 ft✔✔Stages, concerts, trade shows
P4.8 – P516 ft +✔✔Billboards, jumbotrons, signage

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an LED video wall cost?

Pricing depends on size, pixel pitch, and indoor/outdoor rating. Complete turnkey indoor walls start around $14,900 installed; individual panels start around $610. Request a quote and we'll price your exact wall same-day.

What pixel pitch should I choose?

Multiply the pitch (mm) by roughly 3.3 to get the minimum comfortable viewing distance in feet. Audience 8 ft away? P2.5 is ideal. 16+ ft away (outdoor signage)? P4.8 saves budget with no visible quality loss.

Do you install outside of Florida?

Yes — we deliver and install nationwide. Our headquarters is in Pompano Beach, FL, and our install crews travel throughout the US.

Can I rent instead of buy?

Absolutely. We maintain a large rental inventory for events of every size, and offer rent-to-own programs if you want to apply rental fees toward a purchase.

What's included in the warranty?

All new screens include a 5-year manufacturer warranty covering panels, power supplies, and receiving cards — backed by US-stocked spare parts and 24/7 technical support.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer flexible financing options for businesses, churches, and nonprofits. Ask your VertexLED rep for current terms.
