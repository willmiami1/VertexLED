Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an LED video wall cost?

Pricing depends on size, pixel pitch, and indoor/outdoor rating. Complete turnkey indoor walls start around $14,900 installed.

What pixel pitch should I choose?

Multiply the pitch by roughly 3.3 to get the minimum comfortable viewing distance in feet.
